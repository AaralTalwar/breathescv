    About the data

    Air-quality values come from Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), which combines CAMS and other atmospheric models with monitoring and satellite data. They are model estimates, updated hourly, not the EPA's official monitor readings. For the official value for your ZIP, open airnow.gov.

    The US Air Quality Index (AQI) runs from 0 to 500. Each pollutant gets its own index and the overall AQI is the highest of them; the app names that pollutant. PM2.5 is fine particles (wildfire smoke, winter inversions), PM10 is coarse dust (high-desert wind), and ozone peaks on hot afternoons.

    BreatheSCV never shows a Green flag from a reading more than 3 hours old.

    About the thresholds

    Decisions follow the EPA Air Quality Flag Program's Air Quality and Outdoor Activity Guidance for Schools (EPA-456/F-14-003). Read the EPA chart.

    Privacy

    No accounts. No analytics. Your ZIP or location is used to fetch air quality and is stored on this device only if you tap Save. Your action plan and symptom log never leave this device. The only network requests are to the air-quality service, the ZIP look-up, and the map tiles.
